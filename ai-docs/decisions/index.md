## Overview

Architectural Decision Records (ADRs) capture key cross-repo design decisions that shape the OpenShift platform. These document the "why" behind fundamental architectural choices.

## ADR Format

Each ADR follows:
1. **Title** — short decision statement
2. **Context** — what forces led to this decision
3. **Decision** — what was decided
4. **Consequences** — what follows from this decision
5. **Rationale** — why this choice over alternatives
